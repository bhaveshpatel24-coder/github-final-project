# Simple Interest Calculator

## Overview

A simple interest calculator determines the interest earned or paid on a principal amount when interest is calculated only on that original amount. It does not include compounding.

## Formula

```text
Simple Interest = (Principal x Annual Rate x Time) / 100
Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest
```

Enter the annual rate as a percentage and time in years. For a period measured in months, convert it to years by dividing the number of months by 12.

## Inputs

- **Principal:** The initial amount of money.
- **Annual rate:** The yearly interest rate, expressed as a percentage.
- **Time:** The duration of the investment or loan, in years.

## Example

For a principal of 10,000 at an annual rate of 5% for 2 years:

```text
Simple Interest = (10,000 x 5 x 2) / 100 = 1,000
Total Amount = 10,000 + 1,000 = 11,000
```
